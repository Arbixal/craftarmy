> **Status:** open

## The question

Should knowledge-first primarily mean unlocking knowledge activities, collecting
first-craft points, or choosing builds that maximize ongoing knowledge? What gold,
Moxie, concentration, and specialization tradeoffs are acceptable?

### 2026-11-05 — opened

The user requested prioritizing maximum knowledge and both a cheaper stopping point
and maximum-skill continuation. The assistant proposed three targets:
knowledge-ready, budget stop, and max skill. Their ordering and cost constraints are
recommendations, not settled decisions. Maximum skill must not be presented as a
fully completed knowledge tree. Specialization-dependent branches should explain
tradeoffs rather than silently prescribe a build.

price is not the default. Preserve the intended build; default routes avoid required
specialization spending, while optional branches explain the investment and tradeoff

These policy choices → promoted to D06. The thread remains open for affordable-cost
rules, route target definitions, and currency/concentration tradeoffs. Do not ask the
